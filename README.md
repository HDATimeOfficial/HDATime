## What is HDATIME?

HDATIME is the official project name and domain of the Tiempo HDA (HDAT) token. Native token of the HDA Nexus ecosystem, designed under the principle that **time is the most valuable asset**. HDAT is the verifiable digital asset on BNB Smart Chain that backs the entire ecosystem.

**HDAT is NOT an investment, security, share or financial instrument. It does not promise profit, interest or returns.**

### Purpose
To quantify the value of time. It is not a generic utility token. It is a scarce asset (1,000,000), costly and exclusive that converts every minute of focus, management and execution into tangible and verifiable value.

### Vision
To be the tokenized time standard applicable to any industry where time is the asset: real estate, transport, logistics, professional services, consulting, education, health, tourism and fleets.

**Formula: Transformation + Time + Value + Strategy**
